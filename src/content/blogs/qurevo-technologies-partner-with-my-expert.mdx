## 🌍 Two Founders. One Mission.

Although both companies operate in different domains, their long-term vision has always been remarkably similar. They believe Kashmir deserves <Highlight color="blue">modern digital products built with international standards</Highlight> while staying deeply connected to local communities. That shared philosophy naturally led to this collaboration.

**Qurevo Technologies**, founded by **Haadi Sabzar Lone**, has rapidly become known for developing modern business websites, scalable web applications, AI-powered automation systems, and high-performance digital experiences for organizations across Jammu & Kashmir.

On the other hand, **MyExpert**, founded by **Musaib Manzoor**, is building a platform that simplifies one of life's everyday challenges—finding trusted professionals for home services quickly, securely, and conveniently.

One company focuses on building powerful digital infrastructure. The other focuses on making everyday services easier to access. Together, they create an ecosystem where technology directly improves people's daily lives.
